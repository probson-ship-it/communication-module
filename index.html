Department-Specific Focus
Same feature • Different angles

Everyone sees the same feature, but they each need a different story to tell. Training on Monday is split accordingly.

Sales
Uses the feature as a proof point that we help cities close enforcement gaps and create predictable communication between AHJs and service providers.
Customer Success
Needs every answer AHJs will ask: configuration, rollout impact, and how this improves report quality and compliance rates.
Customer Experience
Front line for service providers. Messaging focuses on where to click, what changed on their screen, and why this helps them respond faster.
Data & Product
Owns behavior tracking, edge cases, and logging issues that could impact enforcement or create confusion inside The Compliance Engine.
