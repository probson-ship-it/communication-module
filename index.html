Sequence
Launch Timeline – Week One
Simple, paced, easy to follow

We start with our own teams, then move to AHJs and service providers, and finally bring it into our broader narrative. Every step points back to one idea – clearer communication to close compliance gaps.

Monday
Internal Training – One Day Only
All departments
What the feature does How to explain it simply
  • Sessions for Sales, CSMs, Customer Experience, Data, and Product.
  • Walkthrough of how AHJs and service providers will actually use it.
  • Set clear guidance on “If you’re asked to turn it off, here’s what to say.”
Tuesday
AHJ Teaser Email
External – AHJs only
Short and direct Sets expectation
  • “You’ll soon be able to message service providers directly in TCE.”
  • Frames it as a cleaner way to clarify reports and move corrections faster.
  • Light mention that this is another tool to communicate with their community if they choose.
Thursday
Full Launch Emails – AHJ + Service Providers
External – Email + social
How it works What changes for them Where to go in TCE
  • AHJ launch email: “You can now start a message with service providers from inside TCE instead of chasing email threads.”
  • Service provider launch email: “If you have questions or aren’t sure who to ask, go into TCE – you can reach your AHJ directly there.”
  • Social post reinforces the story: less messy email, fewer calls, more clarity inside the platform.
Friday
Internal Follow-Up & Questions
Internal only
Support questions “Turn it off” requests
  • Teams expect questions from AHJs and service providers, including requests to disable the feature.
  • Guidance is to keep communication inside TCE and explain why it’s better than scattered emails and calls.
  • Collect patterns in questions to inform future tweaks and help content.
Internal Alignment
How Each Team Talks About It
Same feature – different lens

The feature is the same, but the angle isn’t. Each team needs a simple way to explain it that fits their conversations.

Sales
Uses it as proof that BRYCER is serious about closing the loop between AHJs and service providers – less guesswork, more complete follow-up, and fewer compliance gaps slipping through.
Customer Success
Needs clear answers to what AHJs will see, how to start a message, and what to say when an AHJ is worried about volume or wants to turn it off.
Customer Experience
Front line for service providers. Their message is simple: “If you’re not sure who to ask, log into TCE and start there – your AHJ is now reachable in the same place you upload reports.”
Data & Product
Own understanding of usage and edge cases – watching for anything that might slow down communication or create confusion that affects compliance.
