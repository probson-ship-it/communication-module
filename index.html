Launch timeline – week one

We start with our own teams, then move to AHJs and service providers.

Monday · Dec 8
Internal team training
Sales · CSM · Customer Experience · Data · Product
How messaging works What customers will see
  • Walkthrough of the new messaging experience inside TCE.
  • Each department reviews how this shows up in their world and talks through use cases in training.
  • Set clear guidance: if questions come in after launch.
Tuesday
AHJ teaser email
External – AHJ segment
Short and energetic Sets expectation
  • “Something new is coming inside TCE – a simple way to connect directly with service providers when you need clarity.”
  • Positions this as a modern, expected capability that we’re now bringing into TCE, alongside what they already rely on us for.
  • Holds back detail for Thursday to keep attention and interest.
Thursday
AHJ launch email
External – AHJ segment
How it works in TCE Why it helps Optional alerts
  • Explains that AHJs can now message service providers directly inside TCE to clear up questions and move work forward faster.
  • Frames it as a quick way to get to the root of an issue and support safer outcomes – not as extra work.
  • Mentions that AHJs can subscribe to email notifications if they want an alert when someone messages them in TCE, so they don’t have to keep checking manually.
Thursday
Service provider launch email
External – SP segment
Where to go Who they reach Optional alerts
  • “If you have questions or aren’t sure what to do next, you can now go straight into TCE and message your AHJ there.”
  • Emphasizes that conversations live alongside their reports and history, so there’s less confusion and fewer misrouted emails.
  • Mentions that they can turn on email notifications to know when an AHJ has messaged them, instead of guessing or waiting.
Thursday
Social post
External – public
High-level Safety-focused
  • Announces that BRYCER is rolling out built-in messaging between AHJs and service providers inside The Compliance Engine.
  • Keeps the focus on clearer, faster conversations around life safety work.
Friday
Internal follow-up & questions
Internal – all teams
Normalize questions Reinforce direction
  • Internal note that questions and some “can you turn this off?” feedback are normal with any change that touches communication.
  • Guidance is to keep conversations inside TCE and remind customers that this is about getting answers faster, not making things harder.
  • Teams share patterns in questions to inform tweaks and help content going forward.
Team training
Monday · December 8 – internal session
Shared understanding first

On Monday, December 8, we’ll host a full team training on the new communication module in TCE. Each department will walk through how this shows up in their world and talk through real examples.

Purpose
Make sure everyone understands what’s changing, what AHJs and service providers will see, and how to guide questions back into TCE after launch.
What this is not
This is not about scripting every word or locking in detailed talk tracks. It’s about clarity and confidence before we email customers.
Outcome we want
When questions come in, internal teams are aligned on the message: this is a faster, clearer way to resolve issues and keep work moving – inside TCE.
Next steps
After training, we’ll share a short internal recap so everyone has the same baseline explanation to lean on.
