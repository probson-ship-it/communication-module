TCE Conversations Feature Rollout Plan

Objective: Roll out the new Conversations functionality in a controlled, department-aligned sequence that prepares internal teams first, notifies AHJs early, launches jointly to AHJs and Service Providers, and announces the feature to prospects only after stability is confirmed.

Monday, December 8 - Internal Team Training (One-Day, Department-Specific)

Each department receives a tailored session based on their use case and responsibilities.

Sales Team

  • How to position Conversations in demos and proposals
  • How it supports faster compliance and stronger AHJ - Service Provider connection
  • Incorporating it into competitive talking points

Customer Success (CSMs)

  • Full walkthrough of the feature in TCE
  • Expected AHJ questions and approved responses
  • Sensitive AHJ strategies and escalation guidance

Customer Experience / Support

  • How Service Providers will use the feature
  • How to answer Service Provider functionality questions
  • Understanding differences between AHJ and Service Provider views

Data Quality, Engineering, Product

  • Technical functionality and system behavior
  • Logging, visibility, and record history
  • Monitoring adoption and collecting early feedback

Tuesday, December 9 - AHJ Teaser Email

Audience: All active AHJ users in TCE and AHJ segment in Constant Contact.

Purpose:

  • Prepare AHJs that a communication enhancement is coming
  • Reduce surprise and prevent escalations
  • Reinforce that this strengthens community safety

Message: No workflow changes; simply an improvement to help resolve issues faster.

Thursday, December 11 - Launch Day (Joint Announcement)

AHJ Launch Email

  • Announces Conversations as live
  • Shows where to start conversations and where to find messages
  • Reinforces how this supports faster compliance and safer communities

Service Provider Launch Email

  • Simple, supportive announcement
  • Emphasizes clearer communication with AHJs
  • Highlights reduced delays and improved follow-up

In-TCE "What’s New" Message on Message Board

  • Brief on-login notification
  • Reinforces purpose and location of new feature

Social Media Announcement

  • Public visibility
  • Positions BRYCER as driving innovation in fire and life-safety compliance
  • Reinforces safety messaging

Friday, December 12 - Follow-Up and Stabilization

Audience: Internal CSM, Support, Data Quality, Engineering.

Purpose:

  • Address early AHJ and Service Provider questions
  • Document feedback
  • Confirm functionality is operating as expected
  • Identify any jurisdictions needing direct follow-up

End of Quarter - Prospect Newsletter Announcement

Audience: Prospective AHJs, prospects in pipeline, demo participants, and general marketing list.

Placement: "New Features" section of quarterly newsletter.

Purpose:

  • Introduce Conversations as part of ongoing platform innovation
  • Reinforce commitment to strengthening compliance and community safety
  • Leverage the feature as a value-add for prospects evaluating TCE

Notes: Prospects see it only after stabilized release, ensuring no early questions or instability impact pipeline.

Messaging Principles

  • Focus on community safety and faster issue resolution
  • Keep explanations simple and benefit-driven
  • Avoid implying workflow changes
  • Build confidence and trust
  • Maintain consistency across internal and external communications
